## Overview
A quiet personal page with an oversized introduction and a geometric botanical mark. The project itself is the first item in the portfolio.

## Colors
Paper background, dark ink for headings, green for the main action and decorative geometry. Muted green-gray supports descriptions.

## Typography
Self-hosted Manrope for body and display. Lora italic highlights one word in the introduction. Display sizes range from 40px on small screens to 76px on desktop.

## Layout
1280px maximum page width. Desktop introduction has two columns; mobile uses one. Horizontal padding is 64px, 28px below 800px, and 22px below 560px. The project is an open row separated by rules.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat surfaces with thin dividers and no shadows.

## Shapes
Eight elliptical petals and an orbital circle. Buttons have 5px corners.

## Components
The primary link is green with a light label and drawn arrow. Links use visible focus outlines and underlines on hover. Motion is confined to the decorative mark and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Do's and Don'ts
Use verified public profile details. Keep the project row and its source link usable on narrow screens. Avoid invented professional claims.
